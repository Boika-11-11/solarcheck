About SolarCheck

Helping South Africans avoid bad solar installs.

Why this exists

A home solar system costs anywhere from R50 000 to R250 000. That is a lot of money to hand to someone you found on Facebook. The boom in backup power brought a lot of good installers into the market — and a lot of chancers too. Homeowners have been left with unsafe wiring, missing paperwork, and deposits that vanished.

SolarCheck exists so you can see what other homeowners actually experienced before you sign anything.

What the VERIFIED badge means

A green VERIFIED badge means we have checked that the installer holds valid credentials — a PV GreenCard, and registration with the Department of Employment and Labour for electrical work.

It does not mean we guarantee their work. It means their paperwork is real. Always read the reviews too, and always insist on a Certificate of Compliance when the job is done.

How reviews work

Anyone can write a review. Every review is read by a human before it goes live. We remove anything abusive, clearly fake, or not about actual work done. We do not remove a review just because a business does not like it — but businesses can contact us for a right of reply.

How we make money

SolarCheck is free for homeowners, always. When you request quotes, we pass your details to up to three installers who may pay us a referral fee. That never affects star ratings or the order companies appear in — ratings come from reviews only.

Before you sign anything

Ask for their PV GreenCard number. Ask for references from jobs in your suburb. Never pay 100% upfront — milestone payments protect you. Make sure the quote includes the Certificate of Compliance.
