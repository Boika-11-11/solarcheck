Privacy policy

How SolarCheck handles your personal information.

Who we are

SolarCheck is a South African directory and review platform for solar installers. We are the responsible party for the personal information described below, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you write a review: your name, email address, star rating and review text. Your name and review appear publicly once approved. Your email is never shown publicly — we keep it so we can verify a review if it is disputed.

When you request quotes: your name, phone number, email address, city, province, property type, rough budget and any notes you add.

Why we collect it

Reviews help other homeowners choose an installer. Quote requests are passed to installers so they can send you a quote — that is the whole purpose of the form, and we ask for your explicit consent before you submit it.

Who we share it with

Quote request details are shared with up to three solar installers matched to your area and project. We do not sell your information to anyone else, and we do not share review email addresses with anyone.

How long we keep it

Published reviews stay on the site until you ask us to remove them. Quote requests are kept for 24 months so we can resolve any dispute about a referral, then deleted.

Your rights under POPIA

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent to us sharing your quote request. Contact us and we will action it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Reviews and fairness

Every review is checked before it is published. We remove reviews that are abusive, clearly fake, or not about actual work done. If you are a business and believe a review about you is false, contact us and we will investigate — and we will give you a right of reply.

Contact

For any privacy request or complaint, email projectssere@gmail.com.
